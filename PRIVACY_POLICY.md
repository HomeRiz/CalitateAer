## 5. Data Protection Principles

MMAP applies the six GDPR principles for processing personal data:

1. **Lawfulness, fairness, and transparency**
2. **Purpose limitation** — data is collected only for specified, explicit, and legitimate purposes
3. **Data minimisation** — only data adequate and relevant to the stated purpose is processed
4. **Accuracy** — data is kept accurate and up to date
5. **Storage limitation** — data is kept no longer than necessary for the stated purpose
6. **Integrity and confidentiality** — data is secured against unauthorised access and accidental loss

By submitting personal data, members declare and unconditionally accept that their data may be stored and archived in the MMAP database and used for the purposes stated above, in accordance with applicable data protection law.

## 6. Cookie Policy

**What are cookies?**  
Cookies are IT data (including text files) stored on the user's device for the purpose of using the application. They allow the user's device to be recognised and content to be presented in a relevant way adapted to user preferences. Cookies typically contain the name of the originating web page, the date of storage, and a unique number.

**Why are cookies used?**  
Cookies are used to adapt web page content to user preferences, optimise web pages, and prepare anonymous aggregate statistics that help improve the structure and content of the site, without personally identifying the user.

**Types of cookies used:**  
Session cookies — temporary files that remain on the user's device until the session ends or the application is closed.

**Do cookies contain personal data?**  
Personal data collected through cookies is only collected to facilitate certain functionalities. The data is encrypted in a way that makes unauthorised access impossible.

**Deleting cookies:**  
Most browsers allow cookies to be saved by default. These settings can be changed to block automatic cookie management or to notify the user each time cookies are sent to their device. Limiting cookies may affect certain website functionalities.
