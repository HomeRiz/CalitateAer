## II. General Provisions

1. The Platform makes air quality datasets from RNMCA monitoring stations publicly available, free of charge, on request, through the API.
2. The Service can only be accessed after registering and creating an Account on the Platform.
3. Registering on the Platform implies full acceptance by the Beneficiary of these Terms and Conditions.
4. API functions can only be accessed using Authentication Credentials (username and password).
5. An Account and credentials are obtained by submitting a request via the contact form at [www.calitateaer.ro](https://www.calitateaer.ro/public/webservice-page/), including:
   - Full name (for natural persons) or company name (for legal persons)
   - Personal numeric code (for natural persons) or company registration code
   - Email address
   - Reason for requesting API access
   - Which API module(s) are intended to be used
   - Preferred language for data delivery (Romanian or English)
   - Acceptance of these Terms and Conditions and the Privacy Policy
6. After the request is submitted, MMAP will review it and respond within **10 calendar days**. If approved, Authentication Credentials will be sent to the applicant by email.

1. The Service includes:
   - Access to public datasets available on the Platform through the API
   - A licence to use the datasets as described in Section IV

2. Authentication Credentials are generated individually per Beneficiary. **Beneficiaries must not share their credentials with third parties.** Sharing credentials is a violation of these Terms and may result in the deletion of the Account and revocation of API access.

3. Each Beneficiary may hold only one Account and one set of credentials. Using another Beneficiary's credentials is not permitted.

4. Beneficiaries must not exceed the rate limits associated with their Account. Limits are communicated at the time credentials are issued and cover requests per second, per minute, per hour, and per day. Exceeding a limit results in temporary access suspension until the next time cycle. Persistent disregard for rate limits may result in temporary or permanent account suspension.

5. In the event of a Terms violation, the Provider reserves the right to deactivate or delete the Beneficiary's Account.

6. Beneficiaries are fully responsible for their use of the credentials and the data obtained through the API, including actions taken by persons who obtain access to the credentials as a result of the Beneficiary's negligence.

## V. Use of RNMCA Data

Any party using RNMCA data must present it reliably and is specifically required to:

- Clearly and unambiguously indicate to all users that:
  - The **source of the data is RNMCA**
  - The data was **obtained free of charge through the API**
  - The data is **informational and unofficial**
- Provide the **exact measurement time** for all values used
- **Not modify the presented data**; any processing applied must be described intelligibly
- Clearly indicate the **air quality scale** used for determining air quality levels
- Ensure the information is **not distorted or presented in a misleading manner**
